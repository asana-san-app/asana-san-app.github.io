Asana-san privacy policy

Asana-san is used only by its owner. It reads only the command emails the owner sends to themselves in their own Gmail account, sends their text to Anthropic's Claude API for interpretation, and creates or updates tasks in the owner's Asana workspace.

Records are kept only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than those services as needed to carry out the owner's commands.
